REWARD DESIGN
----------------------------------------
  +0.30 per HP dealt to player   (primary aggression signal)
  -0.05 per HP taken from player (light penalty to avoid suicidal play)
  +0.003 x proximity delta       (shaping: move toward player)
  +0.15 per step within ATK range (stay-close-and-fight bonus)
  +3.0  collect target before player
  +20.0 terminal: NPC kills player (win)
  -12.0 terminal: NPC dies (lose)
  +0.002 per step survived        (survival bonus)

ARCHITECTURE
----------------------------------------
  Algorithm   : PPO (Proximal Policy Optimisation)
  Policy net  : MLP [128, 128]  (2 hidden layers)
  Obs space   : Box(14,)  continuous, normalised
  Action space: MultiDiscrete([9, 2])  — movement x attack
  Parallel envs: 4 (DummyVecEnv)
  n_steps=2048, batch_size=64, lr=3e-4, gamma=0.99

KEY FINDINGS
----------------------------------------
  1. Reward shaping is critical: first attempt (200k steps) yielded
     0% win rate because player DPS (40) far exceeded NPC DPS (15).
     After rebalancing to NPC 33 DPS vs player 19 DPS, the agent
     achieved its first win at episode 11 (~64k steps).

  2. The agent learned a hit-and-run strategy: survive the full
     episode timeout (5400 steps / 90s) while dealing incremental
     damage. Most episodes run to timeout rather than ending via kill,
     explaining moderate win % despite healthy episode rewards.

  3. Training reward grew from ~-15 (dying every episode) to +40-45,
     showing genuine policy improvement. The trained agent consistently
     outperforms random on all four evaluation metrics.
